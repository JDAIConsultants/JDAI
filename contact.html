When to reach out

The strongest inquiries usually come from firms that know they want to use AI more intentionally but do not want to rely on guesswork, vendor marketing, or informal internal habits.

  • You need help evaluating a tool. You want to understand retention, training use, confidentiality posture, and whether the product actually fits your workflow.
  • You need governance structure. You want practical guardrails for what can be entered, how outputs are reviewed, and how staff should use AI day to day.
  • You want implementation guidance. You are trying to decide where AI helps, where it should be limited, and how to roll it out without damaging trust.

Best-fit clients

  • Solo attorneys and small firms exploring responsible AI adoption
  • Legal teams comparing multiple AI tools before committing
  • Organizations seeking training, speaking, or governance guidance
